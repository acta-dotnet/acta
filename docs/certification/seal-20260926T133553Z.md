## The rate contract at scale

`metered` is a job whose body writes one witness note and returns; its only policy is
`RateLimit = "600/m"`, ten admissions a second with a burst of ten. The contract is the upper
envelope: in any window of T seconds the meter admits at most `R*(T + W) + B` handler starts, with W
the one-second validity of a booked turn, so 30 in any second and 120 in any ten seconds. Across
10,000 admissions the busiest second held 20 and the busiest ten seconds 109. The previous
million-job run on this engine, on the tree before the meter read its clock after the bucket row's
lock, read 46 in one second; that finding is what the certified commit fixes.

## Scope

Single namespace, single machine, one provider. Multi-namespace isolation at scale remains the
ensemble gate's claim at its own shape. The SQL Server container's memory limit was raised from the
repository compose file's 4 GB for this run: at 4 GB the engine ran out of memory under 1,536
executor slots before any Acta check could run. The checks are `anvil/Anvil/certify.sql` bound to
`certv1tms` plus the `rate-contract` window scan in `CertifyVerdict`; re-run `anvil certify`
against that schema to reproduce the verdict.
