## Namespace isolation, finally falsifiable

Every earlier seal noted the same limit: with one namespace in the schema, `namespace-isolation`
passes by construction. This run removes the caveat. `cert-b` was not an empty row — it registered
76 workers, ran its recurring system slots plus Anvil's pulse, and recorded its own execution
events (26 of them, beside `cert-a`'s 14,038), all while every participant killed workers every
five seconds. The check compares each execution event's namespace against its owning job's across
the whole schema: **zero rows**. No worker in either namespace ever emitted an event for work it
did not own, under contention that included a second live namespace to get wrong.

## The double-spend claim, non-vacuous this time

The three single-participant runs sealed tonight all reported `at-most-once = 0` with zero
interrupted charges — vacuously true. Here the kills landed: of 400 charges, **38 were interrupted
mid-body** and terminalized as ambiguous rather than re-run, 362 succeeded, and the witness notes
show **no charge body ever ran twice** (maximum bodies per job: 1). That is the contract stated
exactly: zero or one executions, never two, with an honest "this may have happened once" instead
of a confident second charge.
