## The rate contract at scale

`metered` is a job whose body writes one witness note and returns; its only policy is
`RateLimit = "600/m"`, ten admissions a second with a burst of ten. The contract is the upper
envelope: in any window of T seconds the meter admits at most `R*(T + W) + B` handler starts, with W
the one-second validity of a booked turn, so 30 in any second and 120 in any ten seconds. Across
10,000 admissions the busiest second held 18 and the busiest ten seconds 108.

## Scope

Single namespace, single machine, one provider. Multi-namespace isolation at scale remains the
ensemble gate's claim at its own shape. This is the second million-job run on this commit and
provider: the first held every property but `no-stranded-work`, which caught one `sys.alerts` fire a
live worker had begun two seconds before the verdict ran, under a lease with three minutes left, and
which the harness then killed on exit. The check counts in-flight system slots while the harness
workers are still alive, a harness race rather than a ledger fault, and the run was repeated rather
than argued. The checks are `anvil/Anvil/certify.sql` bound to `certv1upg` plus the `rate-contract`
window scan in `CertifyVerdict`; re-run `anvil certify` against that schema to reproduce the verdict.
