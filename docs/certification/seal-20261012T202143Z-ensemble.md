The crash workload carries every property a kill can break: durable steps, an AtMostOnce charge, three rate meters side by side at 10/s, 50/s, and 100/s, each checked against its own budget, and laned jobs ten to a lane. Charges, meters, and lane heads are enqueued at High and come due inside the chaos window, so kills land in them; `chaos-by-shape` counts the attempts a kill interrupted in each. `lane-order` and `lane-drained` read the order and the draining from the execution events and runtimes the engine wrote, with no clock.
