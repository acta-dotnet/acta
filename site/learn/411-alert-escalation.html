How to escalate an alert as failures add up

Automatic failure alerts escalate in stages: the first failure, the failure count reaching AlertFailureThreshold, and the final failure when the retry budget is spent.

A how-to for Acta, durable execution for .NET. New to Acta? Run your first durable job in five minutes.

2Read each stage as an alert of its own kind

The sys.alerts sweep writes them once a minute, so they appear after the failures, not with them.

Remember

  • The name in [Job("unstable-report")] and the namespace in Run<AlertEscalationJobs>("alert-escalation") are stored with every job. Treat both as permanent once jobs exist under them: a job stored under a name no handler carries stays Ready, and nothing runs it.
  • UnstableReport is stored with each job as its input. Add a field as optional or with a default. A renamed, removed or retyped field needs a new job name such as unstable-report-v2, and the old handler stays registered until its jobs have finished: Change a job's input without breaking the jobs already stored.
