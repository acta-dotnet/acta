Remember

  • Between two attempts a job waits in the database under its name. The name in [Job("poll-resource")] and the namespace in Run<RescheduleJobs>("reschedule") are stored with every job. Treat both as permanent once jobs exist under them: a job stored under a name no handler carries stays Ready, and nothing runs it.
  • PollResource is stored with each job as its input. Add a field as optional or with a default. A renamed, removed or retyped field needs a new job name such as poll-resource-v2, and the old handler stays registered until its jobs have finished: Change a job's input without breaking the jobs already stored.
