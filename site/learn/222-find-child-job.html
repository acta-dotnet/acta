How to find the child job that handles one item

A child's name on the reads is its idempotency key, {map}/{key} for a child that a map started. List the parent's children and pick the one that carries the item's key.

A how-to for Acta, durable execution for .NET. New to Acta? Run your first durable job in five minutes.

1Start each child under the item's own key

itemKey names each child after its item. The name is stored as the child's idempotency key, and it is how a replay of the parent and a reader from outside find the same child.

2List the parent's children

Parent restricts the job list to the children of one job. Each child shows the name it was started under.

3Pick the child that carries the item's key

The key is the one the map was given for that item, so a page that shows invoice 7 gets from the invoice to its job, and from there to what the job did.

Remember

  • A child's name on the job reads is its IdempotencyKey: the call's name for EnqueueChildAsync, {map}/{key} for a map's child.
  • Keys keep their case: Invoice/7 and invoice/7 are two keys, so build the key the same way where you start the child and where you look for it.
  • The list answers a page at a time, fifty jobs unless PageSize says otherwise and a hundred at most. A parent with more children is read page by page, as in list and page through jobs.
  • A failed child does not fail its parent by itself, so a parent that reads succeeded can still have a child that failed. Handle child jobs that fail shows the parent's side.
  • "invoice" is how send-invoices finds the child for each item. A renamed child is new work: every invoice is sent again.
  • A parent and its children are each stored under their own job name. The names in [Job("send-invoices")] and [Job("send-invoice")], and the namespace in Run<FindChildJobJobs>("find-child-job"), are stored with every job. Treat them as permanent once jobs exist under them: a job stored under a name no handler carries stays Ready, and nothing runs it.
  • SendInvoices is stored with each job as its input. Add a field as optional or with a default. A renamed, removed or retyped field needs a new job name such as send-invoices-v2, and the old handler stays registered until its jobs have finished: Change a job's input without breaking the jobs already stored.

Check yourself

A parent mapped invoices 5, 6 and 7 under the name invoice. Which idempotency key does the child for invoice 6 carry?

Show the answer

invoice/6. The name the map was given comes first, then the item's key. The job's own name, send-invoice, is not part of it.
